Intended use





[pic]





The device is intended for the automated acquisition and processing of
microscopic images of in vitro human semen samples.


The device generates structured, image-derived data representing observable
characteristics of the samples, to support laboratory-related activities
such as method standardization, workflow consistency control, and internal
reference purposes.


The device does not provide medical interpretation, is not intended to
establish or exclude any clinical condition, and shall not be used as an
independent basis for diagnostic or therapeutic decision-making.


The outputs generated by the device  are  non-decisional  and  auxiliary  in
nature,  and  are  intended  to  be  used  by  qualified  professionals   in
conjunction with other laboratory information and relevant contextual data.

1, 2, or 3. When you pick a value, the system will analyze that many fields

captured fields.

3 HL7


Connect SCA SCOPE with  LIS  (Laboratory  Information  System)  to  transfer
information about samples and patients in a bidirectional way using HL7.


From SCA to LIS


Enable this option to send sample/analysis results using  HL7  from  SCA  to
your LIS.


The transference of information from SCA to LIS can be done every time  user
saves the sample,  otherwise  user  manually  sends  results  with  Transfer
button.


It is required to configure LIS IP, LIS Port, LIS Name  and  LIS  Directory.
In addition, there is the option to use First Name^Last Name order.


From LIS to SCA


Configure SCA SCOPE to receive incoming HL7 messages  from  your  LIS.  This
will enable your LIS to create the sample references  and  patients  in  SCA
systems. Then, these references will automatically appear  in  the  “Pending
list” of analyses for each module of SCA SCOPE.


It is required to configure SCA Port, SCA Name and SCA Directory.
